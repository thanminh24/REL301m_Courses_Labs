## Use Decision

The pages strongly confirm that the local course follows the Alberta/Sutton–Barto sequence.
They do not expose enough detail to uniquely answer any of the 38 questions left unresolved
after the local book pass. Lesson titles alone were treated as topic adjacency, not evidence.
Therefore the supplemental map has `coursera-public: 0`; this avoids inventing support from
gated course content.
